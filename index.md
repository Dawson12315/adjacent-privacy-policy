# Adjacent Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Adjacent is a self-hosted music player app. The app connects to a server that you provide and control.

## Information the app stores on your device

Adjacent may store:

- Your server URL
- Your signed-in session preference
- Playback quality preference
- Temporary playback cache
- Temporary artwork cache
- Downloaded playlist audio for offline playback
- Playlist/library metadata needed for offline playback

Adjacent does not store your password.

## Information handled by your self-hosted server

Depending on how your server is configured, your server may process:

- Username
- Music library metadata
- Playlist data
- Listening history and playback stats
- Artist, album, playlist, and track artwork
- Selected images uploaded as artwork

This data is handled by your own self-hosted Adjacent server.

## Third-party sharing

Adjacent does not sell personal data and does not use advertising tracking.

Adjacent does not share app data with third parties, except when you choose to connect the app to services or servers that you control or configure.

## Network security

Adjacent supports HTTPS connections when your server is configured with HTTPS.

If you connect to a local server over HTTP, that connection may not be encrypted.

## Photos and artwork

Adjacent may ask for access to your photo library so you can select artwork images for artists, albums, and playlists.

Selected images are uploaded to your self-hosted server only when you choose to upload them.

## Data deletion

You can clear temporary cache and downloaded playlist audio from inside the app.

You can delete app data from your device settings.

You can manage or delete library, playlist, artwork, and listening data on your self-hosted server.
